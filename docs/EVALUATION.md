## Summary

Personalisation moves results moderately: the mean Spearman correlation between the personalised and the non-personalised order is 0.51, and 7.1 of the plain-retrieval top 10 survive into the personalised top 10. Most of that movement comes from the program alone: an MS CS student with no courses or interests already gives ρ = 0.51 against plain retrieval, because degree fit takes only the values [0.0, 0.5] (no MS CS core courses in the catalog), which lifts CSCE courses over ECEN courses of similar relevance. Against that program-only profile, each student's courses and interests move the order further (mean ρ = 0.89; per profile 0.83 for near_graduation to 0.94 for first_semester, a spread of 0.10). Feasibility at 10 ranges from 0.02 (first_semester) to 0.29 (near_graduation). On average 5.9 of each top 10 are `not_sure`; 43% of recommendable courses list only classification or approval prerequisites, so the readiness rule cannot reason about them. Of the three score components, interest_match contributes the most (mean 0.293); interest_match 0.293, degree_fit 0.101, readiness_adjust 0.073.
