## Summary

Personalisation moves results a lot: the mean Spearman correlation between the personalised and the non-personalised order is 0.28, and 4.4 of the plain-retrieval top 10 survive into the personalised top 10. Most of that movement comes from the program alone: an MS CS student with no courses or interests already gives ρ = 0.27 against plain retrieval, because degree fit takes only the values [0.0, 0.5] (no MS CS core courses in the catalog), which lifts CSCE courses over ECEN courses of similar relevance. Against that program-only profile, each student's courses and interests move the order further (mean ρ = 0.80; per profile 0.72 for near_graduation to 0.88 for first_semester, a spread of 0.15). Feasibility at 10 ranges from 0.03 (first_semester) to 0.44 (near_graduation). On average 7.2 of each top 10 are `not_sure`; 43% of recommendable courses list only classification or approval prerequisites, so the readiness rule cannot reason about them. Of the three score components, interest_match contributes the most (mean 0.165); interest_match 0.165, degree_fit 0.111, readiness_adjust 0.043.
